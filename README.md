## About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate building end-to-end systems across model development and evaluation, computer vision, document intelligence, recommender systems, full-stack applications, and local AI developer tooling.

I care about measurable results, reproducibility, privacy boundaries, failure handling, and documentation that makes a project understandable to someone other than its author.
